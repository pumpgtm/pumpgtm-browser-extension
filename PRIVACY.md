# Privacy

What is stored: your PumpGTM workspace key, in `chrome.storage.local` on this device only. It is not synced to other devices and is never logged.

What is sent, and where: only when you click the extension, the LinkedIn profile URL and page title of the current tab, and your workspace key as the Authorization header, are sent to `https://app.pumpgtm.com`. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and the extension does not read pages in the background.

Remove the extension to delete the stored key. Full policy: https://pumpgtm.com/privacy-policy. Questions: hello@pumpgtm.com.
